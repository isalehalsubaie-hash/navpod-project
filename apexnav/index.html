THE PROBLEMالمشكلة

A cheap jammer can blind a security drone.

Border and critical-site drones depend on GPS. Under jamming the drone drifts, aborts or is lost. Tactical-grade INS kits and anti-jam fleets cost too much to fit to every aircraft.

HOW IT WORKSكيف تعمل

Four steps, no pilot input.

  1. 1
    Load the map

    Satellite tiles of the patrol area are loaded before take-off.

  2. 2
    Fly on GPS

    GPS-aided INS and pure INS run side by side for the whole flight.

  3. 3
    Learn the drift

    An on-board model learns this drone's own INS drift while GPS is healthy.

  4. 4
    Automatic handover

    GPS jammed: the learned correction and camera map fixes take over at once.
